# 30-Day Enterprise AI Study Plan

## How to use this plan
- Study 60 to 90 minutes per day.
- Spend 40% on reading, 30% on note-making, and 30% on speaking answers aloud.
- Every 5th day, do revision and mock interview practice.

---

- RPA fundamentals

**Topics**

## Daily answer practice template
For every topic, practice in this order:
1. Definition in one sentence
2. Where it is used
3. Why it matters in enterprise systems
4. One design tradeoff
5. One real example

---

## End-of-month outcomes
By the end of 30 days, you should be able to:
- Explain AI, ML, GenAI, automation, and RPA clearly
- Design and defend a RAG architecture
- Explain copilot vs agent decisions
- Discuss governance, risk, and production controls
- Speak like an architect to both interviewers and business stakeholders
